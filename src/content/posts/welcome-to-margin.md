A good publication gives stories enough space. A photograph sets the scene, a headline invites you in, and the words do the rest.

Margin brings writing and photography together in a simple editorial website. This edition is a working example of using Marpos Studio to manage an Astro publication.

## Start with the story

Open this article in Studio and change a sentence. Edit the headline, update the summary or choose a different cover from the media library. The website uses the same Markdown file you are editing.

![Mountain scenery beside a quiet lake](/src/assets/images/library/photo-1476514525535-07fb3b4ae5f1.jpg)

## Give images a purpose

A cover introduces the story. An image within the article can slow the pace and give a detail more room. Write useful alternative text so readers can understand what the photograph adds.

> Make the picture part of the story, and give the story room to breathe.

## From an idea to a published page

1. Edit the story and its details in Marpos Studio.
2. Preview the page in the real Astro website.
3. Review the content and image changes together.
4. Publish the reviewed changes to the repository. A configured hosting service can then rebuild the website.

The original Margin sample stories remain here so you can explore categories, tags, authors and article series. This is demonstration content, rather than a live news service.
